Credit Card Churn Risk Prediction System

A machine learning–based project that predicts the likelihood of a customer discontinuing a bank’s credit card service, helping financial institutions take proactive retention measures.

Design and Development

Aug 2025


This project focuses on building an end-to-end machine learning pipeline to identify customers likely to churn based on transactional and behavioral data. The workflow included data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, and model development using LightGBM and Scikit-learn. A fully modular pipeline was implemented to automate data ingestion, transformation, and model training, following CI/CD and production-level coding practices.


The final model achieved an impressive ROC-AUC score of 0.99, accurately classifying high-risk customers. A Flask-based web application was deployed on Render, allowing real-time churn probability predictions through a user-friendly interface. This solution enables banks to design targeted retention strategies, reduce revenue loss, and improve customer satisfaction.


1. I tuned LightGBM model with Bayesian Search because it uses probabilistic models to intelligently explore the hyperparameter space, balancing exploration and exploitation. An important point here was to define a class_weight hyperparameter, such that the estimator was able to better learn the patterns in minority target class (churn customers).


1. The final LigthGBM performance was excellent. A 0.89 recall indicates that the model correctly identifies 89% of the churners. In practical terms, looking at the confusion matrix, it has accurately predicted 290 out of 325 churners. Furthermore, a 0.90 precision indicates that, out of all customers predicted as churn, 90% of them are actually churners. In practical terms, considering the confusion matrix, out of 324 customers predicted as churn, 297 of them are indeed churners. Finally, the probability scores follow an order, with churners being assigned to higher probabilities, demonstrating that the model outcomes are reliable.

Once I built a model that is able to accurately predict the probability of a customer to churn, I developed a Flask API web app such that the bank can get a customer's probability of churning by giving the input features information, solving the business problem.
